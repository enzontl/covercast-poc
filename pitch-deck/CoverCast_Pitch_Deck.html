Solution

VISION STATEMENT

For restaurant groups whose chefs order by gut feeling and throw away 8 % of their purchases, CoverCast is a forecasting and ordering tool for kitchens that prepares tomorrow’s order every evening and cuts waste to 5 %. Unlike chain platforms and POS stock modules, it is built for table-service groups: bookings, weather and events drive each service’s forecast, and the chef sends the order in 5 minutes.
