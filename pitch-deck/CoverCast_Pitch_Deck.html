02 · The product

Solution

Karim gets tomorrow’s order already computed and sends it in 5 minutes.

Vision statement

For · CoverCast is · that · unlike

For restaurant groups whose chefs order by gut feeling and throw away 8 % of their purchases, CoverCast is a forecasting and ordering tool for kitchens that prepares tomorrow’s order every evening and cuts waste to 5 %. Unlike chain platforms and POS stock modules, it is built for table-service groups: bookings, weather and events drive each service’s forecast, and the chef sends the order in 5 minutes.

07 · The moat

Competition and defensibility

Inpulse and Nory already forecast and order. Our edge is the chef’s screen, a fast setup and a measured pilot.

Alternatives

Notebook and Excel: free, no forecast.

POS stock module: stock levels, no forecast to order.

Inpulse: forecasts and orders for networks of 5 to 1,000+ sites, on quote.

Nory: AI ordering inside a full platform, 199 to 329 EUR.

WHERE OUR IP SITS

  • Build: order orchestration and the chef’s screen, forecasts per restaurant.
  • Buy: pipeline, observability, a small LLM for explanations.
  • Partner: POS providers for data and distribution.
  • Moat: wired into recipes, stock and the 6 pm routine.

Guardrails

GDPR: booking counts only, no names; DPA; EU hosting; sales kept 24 months, logs 12.

EU AI Act: minimal risk, forecasting only.

Human: the chef validates every order; his log never scores him.
