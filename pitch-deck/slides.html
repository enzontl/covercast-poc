02 / 10 48-Hour Deployment · Explainable Forecast · Chef-in-the-Loop

Solution

A single-screen nightly copilot that turns tomorrow’s demand signals into a validated ingredient order in 3 minutes.

CCCoverCast
Product Vision Statement

For multi-site casual-dining Head Chefs and Operations Directors whose gut-feel nightly supplier orders waste 8% of food purchases and 45 minutes after service, CoverCast is a nightly order-preparation copilot that reduces food waste toward 5% by generating probabilistic cover forecasts and ingredient-level supplier carts ready in one click. Unlike heavy all-in-one restaurant ERPs (Nory, Inpulse) requiring months of suite-wide rollout or static inventory trackers (MarketMan, Restoke) blind to weather and neighborhood events, CoverCast plugs into existing POS and booking tools in 48 hours, explains every adjustment in plain kitchen language, and keeps final order sign-off in the Chef’s hands.

Before CoverCast · 45 Min / Night

Manual Post-Service Guesswork

  • 22:45: Export POS sales & scroll Zenchef party counts manually.
  • 23:00: Check smartphone weather app; miss nearby Expo or transit disruption.
  • 23:10: Convert expected covers into kg/crates mentally; add +25% fear buffer.
  • 23:30: Type separate WhatsApp/email orders to 4–5 Rungis suppliers → 8% waste.
After CoverCast · 3 Min / Night

Automated Prep, 1-Click Sign-Off

  • 22:30 (Auto): Syncs POS mix, Zenchef bookings, hourly Météo-France & local events (<600m).
  • 22:45 (2 sec): Quantile model outputs P50 covers + 90% confidence interval & net SKU packs.
  • 22:46: Plain-English brief flags exact weather/event drivers & 24h shelf-life items.
  • 22:48: Karim spot-checks fridge, tweaks ±1 pack if needed, clicks Approve → 5% target.
Human-in-the-Loop Governance

What Stays 100% Under Chef Control

  • Zero autonomous purchasing: Orders never leave without Karim’s explicit 1-click validation.
  • Physical quality overrides: 1-tap − / + pack steppers when fridge produce ripens faster than theoretical stock.
  • Chef’s daily specials: Off-menu slate dishes (ardoise du jour) & private VIP menus added freely.
  • Supplier relationship: Custom notes attached directly to Rungis/butcher POs.
